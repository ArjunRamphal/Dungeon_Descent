Malvel falls before the obsidian throne. His last breath vanishes beneath the rumble of the lava, and the chains above him break. Sophie drops from her cage, her torn dress settling around her feet.

For a moment, she gives you the smile the kingdom has been waiting to see.

"You came for me. I knew you would."

Your weapon stays raised.

"You were never his prisoner."

The smile fades. You lay the damp letter beside the shopkeeper's book, then open her diary to the passages about the weapon. You have heard the wounded butler's account and the creatures' whispered confession. You have seen the vision of Malvel standing before her throne. There is no missing piece left for her to hide behind.

"Eldred deserved to live," you say. "Falkreath deserved better than your father's greed. But the people you meant to burn had no part in his death."

Sophie looks at the letter. Her hand trembles before she closes it into a fist.

"They cheered when my father called him a traitor. They wore the cloth his people wove, ate the grain they grew, and never asked what it cost. Do you think they will listen to a book?"

"They'll listen to the people who survived."

"And he will silence them. He always does."

Behind the throne, a line of fire climbs an obsidian pillar. The floor shudders. Sophie turns towards it, and you recognise the markings from her diary: the weapon's focusing seal. Malvel's death has not destroyed it.

"One command," she whispers. "That's all it takes."

You step between her and the pillar.

"Then let it end here."

For a heartbeat, you think she might stop. Her eyes rest on Eldred's name, visible on the open letter. Then she reaches past you, her fingers bright with molten light, and begins to speak.

You strike before she finishes.

Sophie staggers into your arm. The light leaves her hand. You lower her to the stone as the unfinished command dies on her lips. She searches your face, but there is nothing you can say that will return what either of you has lost.

The princess is dead.

You rise and drive your blade into the focusing seal. Cracks spread through the obsidian. Its gathered fire spills harmlessly into the channels beneath the throne, and the terrible pressure under the floor begins to ease. Far above you, Aethoria sleeps through the dawn it almost never saw.

You gather the letter, the book and the diary. Sophie's crown lies where it fell. You leave it beside her.

There is no rescued princess at your side when you emerge from the dungeon. At the palace gates, the king demands an explanation. You give it before his guards, his courtiers, and the people gathering to welcome their hero home.

You tell them about the weapon. You tell them who commanded Malvel. Then you open the book and tell them what their king did to Falkreath.

The warrior you freed speaks. The butler you saved adds his testimony. When the king orders silence, others ask to hear more.

You accept neither his reward nor his pardon. You place the evidence in the hands of those who have come to listen, and stay to answer their questions.

Sophie will never rule over the ashes of Aethoria. Her father will have to face the living people of Falkreath.

You have brought the kingdom its future. What you could not bring home was its princess.
