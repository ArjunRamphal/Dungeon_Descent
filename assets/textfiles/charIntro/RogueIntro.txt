ASHER — THE ROGUE
Weapon: Throwing Knives

BACKSTORY
Asher was born and raised in the unforgiving slums of the capital. With no family to shelter him, he survived on sharp reflexes, quick wits, and a talent for disappearing into the shadows before corrupt city guards or rival gangs could corner him.

One fateful night, while sneaking into the palace to pilfer a prized gemstone, Asher caught sight of Princess Sophie and was instantly captivated. For the first time, he glimpsed a chance to leave his life of crime behind and become someone worthy of her trust. When Sophie was abducted, Asher saw his moment of redemption — driven by a tangled mix of loyalty, ambition, and the hope of outrunning his troubled past.

CLASS SPECIALTY & TACTICAL ABILITY
• Shadow Step (Passive): Begins with +3 Agility (8 total) and high starting Accuracy (5), gaining 1.5x growth on Agility and Health rewards.
• Primary Combat Stat: Agility powers the Rogue's attacks while granting the largest strike budget per fight.
• Shadow Step (Once per Battle): Strikes for 1.15x Agility damage, completely evades the enemy's counterattack, pierces wards/thorns, and refunds its strike — letting the Rogue dodge lethal boss telegraphs without losing tempo.

HOW STATS WORK IN THE DUNGEON
• Agility — Primary attack damage for the Rogue, and adds floor(Agility / 3) extra strikes per battle.
• Health — Your life pool across all three floors. Reaching 0 in combat or riddles ends the descent.
• Accuracy — Grants an Accuracy / 35 chance to land a 2x critical strike.
• Wisdom — Adds floor(Wisdom / 2) bonus seconds to every timed riddle.
• Observation — Above 30, automatically eliminates one false answer on every riddle.
• Strength — Contributes to your overall journey score and serves as the Warrior's primary weapon stat.
