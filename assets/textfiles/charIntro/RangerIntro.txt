HALT — THE RANGER
Weapon: Hunting Longbow

BACKSTORY
Halt was born and raised in Aethoria's northernmost province, where rugged terrain and unforgiving winters forged him into a peerless hunter and tracker.

As a young man, he enlisted in the kingdom's elite ranger corps, quickly distinguishing himself through uncanny marksmanship and sharp eyes. During a routine patrol, Halt uncovered a dark conspiracy tying corrupt nobles to a shadowy cabal. When he tried to expose the plot, he was framed for treason and forced to flee into the wilderness, leaving his comrades and former life behind.

When rumors of Princess Sophie's abduction reached the wilds, Halt felt an undeniable pull to return. Whether driven by the hope of redemption or the chance to clear his name, he steps into the depths armed with his trusty longbow, his keen wits, and a quiet resolve to see justice served.

CLASS SPECIALTY & TACTICAL ABILITY
• Steady Aim: Begins with +3 Accuracy (8 total) and gains 1.5x growth on Accuracy and Health rewards.
• Primary Combat Stat: Accuracy governs both the Ranger's base attack damage and critical-hit frequency.
• Precision Shot (Once per Battle): Fires a guaranteed critical strike dealing 2.5x Accuracy damage that pierces boss defensive wards — unmatched for finishing off wounded guardians.

HOW STATS WORK IN THE DUNGEON
• Accuracy — Primary attack damage for the Ranger, and grants an Accuracy / 35 chance for 2x critical hits.
• Health — Your life pool across all three floors. Reaching 0 in combat or riddles ends the descent.
• Agility — Adds floor(Agility / 3) extra strikes per battle.
• Wisdom — Adds floor(Wisdom / 2) bonus seconds to every timed riddle.
• Observation — Above 30, automatically eliminates one false answer on every riddle.
• Strength — Contributes to your overall journey score and serves as the Warrior's primary weapon stat.
