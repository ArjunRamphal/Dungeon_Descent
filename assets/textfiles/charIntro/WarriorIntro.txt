CASSIO — THE WARRIOR
Weapon: Dragon-Steel Sword

BACKSTORY
Cassio was born into a poor family of farmers in the outer districts of Aethoria. Though his parents struggled to make ends meet, they instilled in him an unyielding sense of determination and resilience.

From a young age, Cassio demonstrated a natural aptitude for the sword and close-quarters combat, earning a place in the kingdom's army. His discipline and valor soon won him recognition within the King's Guard, where he rose to become a sworn knight.

While serving as Princess Sophie's personal guard, Cassio came to love her deeply, even knowing that his humble origins made him an unlikely suitor. When news arrived that Sophie had been kidnapped by Malvel — the very dark sorcerer who slew Cassio's mentor, Sir Henry — he swore to see justice done. Bearing his dragon-steel blade and the lessons of countless battles, Cassio descends into the dungeon to rescue the princess and prove himself worthy of her heart.

CLASS SPECIALTY & TACTICAL ABILITY
• Runic Might: Begins with +3 Strength (7 total) and gains 1.5x growth on Strength and Health rewards.
• Primary Combat Stat: Strength governs the Warrior's base attack damage.
• Shield Bash (Once per Battle): Delivers a 1.35x Strength blow that staggers the enemy and completely blocks its counterattack for that exchange — ideal for shutting down telegraphed boss attacks.

HOW STATS WORK IN THE DUNGEON
• Strength — Primary attack damage for the Warrior.
• Health — Your life pool across all three floors. Reaching 0 in combat or riddles ends the descent.
• Accuracy — Grants an Accuracy / 35 chance to land a 2x critical strike.
• Agility — Adds floor(Agility / 3) extra strikes per battle, extending your strike budget against tough guardians.
• Wisdom — Adds floor(Wisdom / 2) bonus seconds to every timed riddle.
• Observation — Above 30, automatically eliminates one false answer on every riddle.
