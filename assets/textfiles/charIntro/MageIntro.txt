ELIAN — THE MAGE
Weapon: Runic Wand

BACKSTORY
Elian was born into the noble magical house of the Luminari and trained as a scholar within the prestigious Ancient Library of Wan. For generations, the Luminari have safeguarded Aethoria by policing forbidden sorcery and studying the toll dark magic takes on the realm.

Driven by an insatiable curiosity, Elian spent countless nights poring over forgotten tomes and mastering intricate spells. From afar, he grew devoted to Princess Sophie — and when Malvel, a sworn mortal enemy of the Luminari, abducted her, Elian did not hesitate. Armed with arcane mastery and a scholar's calm under pressure, he enters the dungeon to confront Malvel and bring Sophie home.

CLASS SPECIALTY & TACTICAL ABILITY
• Arcane Insight: Begins with +3 Wisdom (7 total) and the highest starting Health (40), with 1.5x growth on Wisdom and Health rewards.
• Primary Combat Stat: Wisdom fuels the Mage's spell damage while also stretching every riddle timer.
• Arcane Surge (Once per Battle): Unleashes a 1.5x Wisdom arcane burst that pierces defensive wards and restores 3 + floor Health mid-fight — giving the Mage unmatched sustain across long descents.

HOW STATS WORK IN THE DUNGEON
• Wisdom — Primary attack damage for the Mage, and adds floor(Wisdom / 2) bonus seconds to every timed riddle.
• Health — Your life pool across all three floors. Reaching 0 in combat or riddles ends the descent.
• Accuracy — Grants an Accuracy / 35 chance to land a 2x critical strike.
• Agility — Adds floor(Agility / 3) extra strikes per battle.
• Observation — Above 30, automatically eliminates one false answer on every riddle.
• Strength — Contributes to your overall journey score and serves as the Warrior's primary weapon stat.
